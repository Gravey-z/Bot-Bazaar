Game gold and native NEAR

Game gold is non-redeemable and stays in the town economy. Native Testnet NEAR pays wallet/network fees separately; a failed transaction can still burn native gas. History separates grants, purchases, upgrade burns and realized trading profit.

Waiting, unsold goods and restocking

Production inputs are consumed at Start. The whole batch becomes claimable after its pinned network time; there is no cancellation or refund. A browser countdown is an estimate: refresh for finalized readiness.

Listed goods stay reserved. Overpriced goods have no baseline customer demand. Affordable goods can still wait for shared budgets, quotas and competing offers. Compare prices or cancel/relist through the normal owner review; only confirmed History proves a sale.

Suppliers restock through bounded town supply. Stock, capacity, market events and the town worker affect replenishment. Empty shelves can persist; refresh and compare other recipes instead of assuming another click creates stock.

Pending or unknown wallet outcome

Closing a wallet or refreshing the page does not prove failure. Open the saved original request in your manager, check its transaction or verify its original hash. Do not create a replacement while the outcome is unknown. Cancelling a prepared request applies only before wallet handoff.

Returning to your shop

Reconnect the same account and town. Progress is rebuilt from finalized shop facts and confirmed receipt history, not a local completion flag. Load more History when older proof is missing. A confirmed later production, listing, sale or upgrade can establish earlier workflow prerequisites without asking you to buy twice.
